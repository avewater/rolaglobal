Who We Serve

Three institutional buyers.
One complete offering.

ROLA's institutional model works across three distinct buyer types. The program adapts to each, the delivery standard does not.

01 Athletic Departments & NIL Collectives
+

Athletic departments are ROLA's highest-priority institutional partner. The program is built specifically for NIL collective and compliance-integrated delivery across full rosters, covering every sport, not just the revenue programs.

What you get
Roster-wide program, compliance-safe activation, quarterly reporting to leadership, co-branded recruiting materials, ROLA brand partner network access.
How it starts
A selective research partnership with your department. Your athletes take part in ROLA research and attend a workshop, and your program receives the findings.
02 Sports Agents & Player Managers
+

Sports agents close representation deals. ROLA builds the business infrastructure behind the clients they represent. The combination is the most complete athlete management offering in the market, elite representation and a business behind the name.

What you get
Stronger client retention, differentiated agency offering, better post-career outcomes, and a revenue share structure on every client ROLA serves.
Structure
Revenue share per client referral, co-branded athlete program, or exclusive agency partner agreement. Structures tailored to roster size and relationship model.
03 Professional Sports Organizations
+

Professional organizations represent the frontier of athlete business development. Post-career outcomes, player retention, and off-field brand architecture are areas where infrastructure investment produces measurable returns, on both the business and the field.

What you get
Player business development program, off-field brand infrastructure, post-career transition systems, and a reporting framework that connects business development to player performance outcomes.
Structure
Custom partnership structure based on roster size, league, and organizational objectives. Both seasonal and multi-year arrangements available.
